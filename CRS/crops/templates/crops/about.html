How It Works
Step 01

Enter field data

Provide soil nutrients (N, P, K), pH, temperature, humidity, and rainfall for your plot. No lab jargon required — just the values you already measure or can estimate.

Step 02

AI matches crops

CropSense compares your conditions against agronomic patterns to rank crops by suitability, so you see the strongest fits first — not a single black-box answer.

Step 03

Act with confidence

Review match probabilities, seasonal context, and your input summary. Use the recommendation to plan planting, inputs, and irrigation with clearer priorities.
